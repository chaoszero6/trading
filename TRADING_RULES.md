# Trading Rules — Multi-Agent System

## Core Principle
**Always combine OI with price action and volume for analysis.**

## Analysis Checklist (every suggestion must check)

### 1. Open Interest (OI)
- Highest Call OI → Resistance level
- Highest Put OI → Support level  
- Rising OI + Rising Price = Long Build-Up (bullish)
- Rising OI + Falling Price = Short Build-Up (bearish)
- Falling OI + Rising Price = Short Covering (bullish reversal)
- Falling OI + Falling Price = Long Unwinding (bearish reversal)

### 2. Price Action
- Support/Resistance from OI must align with price levels
- Breakout above high OI call zone = strong bullish signal
- Breakdown below high OI put zone = strong bearish signal
- False breakout if OI doesn't confirm (price moves but OI drops)

### 3. Volume
- High OI + Low Volume = weak conviction, likely false level
- High OI + High Volume = strong level, respect it
- OI buildup without volume increase = trap

### 4. Risk Rules
- Never trade OI alone — always need price + volume confirmation
- Max 5% capital per position
- Weekly expiry: close by 3:15 PM
- Monthly expiry: close by 3:15 PM on expiry day
- No trades in first 15 min and last 15 min of market
